The Rhythm of a Circle

Drafts are due at the start of the session before your letters date and letters at the start of the letters date; circles meet within the week that follows (Unit 2 meets in class at Session 14). A draft or letters posted late still count until the close, 1:15 pm on the unit's memo date, and are recorded as late; two or more late items across the term cost participation Honors. Your draft and letters are part of the portfolio: if either is missing at the close, the portfolio is not met. The memo is accepted up to one week late and cannot then earn Honors Pass. Memo feedback is aimed at about two weeks after the memo date. The Circle Guide ↗ has every step, the Memo Rubric, and the prompts (Appendix A).
